About Korea Money Desk

Korea Money Desk is an English-language blog for foreign residents, workers, students and long-stay visitors in Korea. Pay, prices, tax and banking in Korea — for people who live here.

Written in Seoul by a long-time resident who reads Korean-language government notices so readers do not have to. Not a tax adviser or financial adviser; every figure links to its official source.

What we use

  • Bank of Korea Economic Statistics System (ECOS) — base rate and consumer prices
  • Exchange-rate data from public market feeds, shown with the date and time it was taken
  • National Tax Service, National Health Insurance Service and HiKorea (Korea Immigration Service) notices
  • Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute public-holiday data via data.go.kr

How we work

  • Every post shows the date its data was taken and the date it was last updated.
  • Every figure links to the page or data series it comes from.
  • We correct mistakes in the post itself and add a dated note.
  • We do not accept paid posts.

We built our own tools for this desk. They gather the day's official notices and public data, draft a post with the help of AI language models, and every draft has to pass checks we wrote — sources, dates, numbers, tone — before it goes out. The topics, the sources we trust and the bar a post has to clear are ours, and we reread what goes out and fix mistakes when we find them. Illustrations marked "Illustration" are made with an image generation tool. If you spot a mistake first, tell us.
