This page explains what information is collected when you visit Korea Entry Desk (koreaentrydesk.blogspot.com) and how it is used. We have tried to keep it short and in plain English.

Information you give us

You can read everything here without signing up. If you send a message through the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message by email. We use them only to reply to you, and we don't sell or share them with anyone.

If you leave a comment, it is public, along with the name or profile you comment under.

Cookies and third-party services

This blog runs on Blogger, a Google service, and uses a few Google tools that may set cookies or collect data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit:

  • Blogger hosts the site and keeps basic logs.
  • Google Analytics helps us see how many people visit and which posts are read. The data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.
  • Google AdSense may show ads on this site.

Images in posts are served from a public GitHub repository through the jsDelivr content delivery network, which may log the requests it serves.

About the ads

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

To learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The blog will still work, though some ads may be less relevant.

Children

This blog is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
