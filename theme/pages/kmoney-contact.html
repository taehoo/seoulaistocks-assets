Spotted a wrong figure? A rule or rate change we missed? We'd like to hear it.

The easiest way to reach us is the contact form on this page. It lands in our inbox. We read everything and usually reply within a few days, Seoul time.

What gets a fast reply

Corrections. Send the post link and what you think is off, ideally with the official page that says so. We'd rather fix a post than be right about it.

Rule or rate changes. If you see a new notice from the National Tax Service, the National Health Insurance Service, the Bank of Korea or HiKorea, send the link.

Topic requests. Pay, prices, tax or banking questions you want explained from the Seoul side.

What doesn't

Your own case. We can't prepare your tax return or tell you which product to choose — please ask the relevant agency or a licensed adviser. See the Disclaimer.

Personal documents. Please don't send ID scans, tax documents or account numbers.

Sponsored posts, paid links, "collaborations." No.

Thanks for reading.
