Who we are

Korea Entry Desk is an independent site written in Seoul. It is not run by, approved by or connected to the Korean government, any embassy, the Korea Immigration Service, Invest KOREA or any visa agency. Every rule we describe comes from a public source, and each post links it.

Not legal or immigration advice

Everything on this site is general information about entering, staying in and doing business in Korea. We are not lawyers, immigration agents or accountants, and we don't know your nationality, history or plans. Rules change, sometimes at short notice. Before you travel, apply or invest, check the date on each post and the linked official page, and confirm with the Korea Immigration Service, a Korean embassy or consulate, Invest KOREA or a qualified adviser.

Sources and summaries

Rules and dates come from official Korean notices, the K-ETA and visa portals and public data, each linked in the post. Korean-language notices are summarized in our own words, not translated word for word. Exchange rates and other figures are shown with the time they were taken and are for reference only. When we find a mistake we fix the post and say so.
