Spotted an outdated rule? A K-ETA or visa change we missed? We'd like to hear it.

The easiest way to reach us is the contact form on this page. It lands in our inbox. We read everything and usually reply within a few days, Seoul time.

What gets a fast reply

Corrections. Send the post link and what you think is off, ideally with the official page that says so. We'd rather fix a post than be right about it.

Rule changes. If you see a new notice from the Korea Immigration Service, an embassy or Invest KOREA, send the link.

Topic requests. Entry steps, first-week setup or business questions you want explained from the Seoul side.

What doesn't

Your own case. We can't review applications or tell you whether you qualify for a visa — please ask the Korea Immigration Service, an embassy or a qualified adviser. See the Disclaimer.

Personal documents. Please don't send passport scans, ID or account numbers.

Sponsored posts, paid links, "collaborations." No.

Thanks for reading.

— The Korea Entry Desk
