Most foreign residents need three things before a Korean bank will open a full account: an alien registration card, a Korean phone number in their own name, and a reason for the account such as a work contract or school enrollment. Banks apply their own checks on top of that, and limits on new accounts are common in the first months.

Overseas transfers are regulated by foreign-exchange rules. Small transfers are routine; larger or frequent ones may need a designated bank and supporting documents. Posts here explain the steps and link to each bank's and regulator's own page.
