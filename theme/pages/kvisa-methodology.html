How we source and check

Korea Entry Desk is built on official and public data. The main sources are:

  • K-ETA official site and Korea Immigration Service notices (English and Korean)
  • Korea Visa Portal (Ministry of Foreign Affairs / Ministry of Justice)
  • Korea Tourism Organization (VisitKorea) for arrival, transport and payment information
  • Public-holiday data via data.go.kr and exchange rates from public market feeds

How numbers get into a post

  • Data is collected on a schedule and stored with the date it was taken.
  • A post quotes a figure only with its date and a link to the source.
  • Official pages we rely on are checked for changes every day; when a page changes, the posts that quote it are reviewed.
  • Drafts are prepared with software tools from this data and checked against the sources before publishing.

What we leave out

  • Rumours and unsourced claims.
  • Individual recommendations.
