Employees in Korea usually settle income tax through their employer in the year-end settlement at the start of each year; people with other income file in May. Foreign residents who stay long enough are generally enrolled in national health insurance, either through work or as regional subscribers.

The details — who qualifies for flat-rate options, which deductions apply, when premiums are due — change from year to year, so posts in this series quote the National Tax Service and National Health Insurance Service pages directly.
