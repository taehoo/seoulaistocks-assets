Privacy policy

This policy explains what happens to information when you visit Korea Money Desk, which is hosted on Google's Blogger platform.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It uses cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type. We do not see your name or contact details. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on or by blocking cookies.

Advertising

Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com).

Comments and the contact form

If you comment or use the contact form, Blogger processes what you send under Google's privacy policy. We use it only to reply or correct a post.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser. If you want a message you sent us deleted, ask through the contact form.
