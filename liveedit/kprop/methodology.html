How we source and check

Korea Property and Rates Desk is built on official and public data. The main sources are:

  • Bank of Korea ECOS: base rate, bank lending rates, government bond yields and the KB housing price indices it publishes
  • Korea Real Estate Board (R-ONE) releases
  • Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT) apartment sale and jeonse transaction records
  • Exchange rates from public market feeds

How numbers get into a post

  • Data is collected on a schedule and stored with the date it was taken.
  • A post quotes a figure only with its date and a link to the source.
  • Official pages we rely on are checked for changes every day; when a page changes, the posts that quote it are reviewed.
  • Drafts are prepared with software tools from this data and checked against the sources before publishing.

What we leave out

  • Rumours and unsourced claims.
  • Individual buy, sell or rent recommendations.
