Spotted a wrong figure? A rate change or a rule we missed? We'd like to hear it.

The easiest way to reach us is the contact form on this page. It lands in our inbox. We read everything and usually reply within a few days, Seoul time.

What gets a fast reply

Corrections. Send the post link and what you think is off, ideally with the official page or data series that says so. We'd rather fix a post than be right about it.

Data and rule changes. If you see a new release from the Bank of Korea, the Korea Real Estate Board or the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport, send the link.

Topic requests. Jeonse, mortgages, rates or buying steps you want explained from the Seoul side.

What doesn't

Your own case. We can't tell you whether to buy, sell or rent, or review a contract — please ask a licensed real-estate agent, a lawyer or a financial adviser. See the Disclaimer.

Personal documents. Please don't send ID, contracts or account numbers.

Sponsored posts, paid links, "collaborations." No.

Thanks for reading.
