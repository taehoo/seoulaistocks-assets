About Korea Property and Rates Desk

Korea Property and Rates Desk is an English-language blog for foreign investors, expats deciding whether to rent or buy, and analysts following Korean housing. Seoul housing, Korean interest rates and the won: official data, read in English.

Written in Seoul from Bank of Korea, Korea Real Estate Board and Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport releases, read in the original Korean. Not a licensed real-estate agent or financial adviser; every figure links to its official page and date.

What we use

  • Bank of Korea ECOS: base rate, bank lending rates, government bond yields and the KB housing price indices it publishes
  • Korea Real Estate Board (R-ONE) releases
  • Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT) apartment sale and jeonse transaction records
  • Exchange rates from public market feeds

How we work

  • Every post shows the date its data was taken and the date it was last updated.
  • Every figure links to the page or data series it comes from.
  • We correct mistakes in the post itself and add a dated note.
  • We do not accept paid posts.

We built our own tools for this desk. They gather the day's official releases and public data, draft a post with the help of AI language models, and every draft has to pass checks we wrote — sources, dates, numbers, tone — before it goes out. The topics, the sources we trust and the bar a post has to clear are ours, and we reread what goes out and fix mistakes when we find them. Illustrations marked "Illustration" are made with an image generation tool. If you spot a mistake first, tell us.
