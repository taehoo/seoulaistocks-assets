The DART Filing on Hyundai Wia

In the DART filing dated October 2, 2026, the National Pension Service confirmed it reduced its Hyundai Wia position to 6.26%[1]. That marked a decrease of 1.05 percentage points from the prior filing date[1].

The trade description logged on DART is "simple acquisition or disposal"[1]. This transaction type covers routine buying or selling on the exchange and does not mean the fund adopted an active posture or changed its core strategy toward Hyundai Wia.

Other large shifts landed on the same disclosure date, including a lift to 9.84% in metals producer Hyundai Steel Company and a drop to 7.21% in software and games publisher Hyundai Autoever. However, the move in Hyundai Wia is notable for how steadily the position has thinned out over consecutive reports.

How Does the Reason Code Function for This Position?

The National Pension Service listed "simple acquisition or disposal" on October 2, 2026 as the formal reason code for trimming 1.05 percentage points in Hyundai Wia[1]. That classification describes the nature of the transaction itself rather than the broader holding purpose on record.

For the holding purpose itself, the pension fund classified its Hyundai Wia investment as a simple investment on March 10, 2026. Under Korean market definitions, a simple investment designates a passive holding, whereas a general investment indicates the fund reserves the right to engage on shareholder governance topics. The fund stepped down from general investment to simple investment in that March report.

A shift in purpose is a signal of intent rather than an action, showing that the fund elected not to keep an active governance reservation. Crucially, public filings cannot tell you which internal asset allocation targets or indexing parameters prompted the subsequent disposals.

Tracing the Pension Fund's Moves in Hyundai Wia

Over the past eighteen months, the National Pension Service has swung its Hyundai Wia holding between 6.26% and 9.37% through seven separate filings. The October 2, 2026 report marks the lowest level recorded across that sequence[1].

The sequence began on May 2, 2025, when the fund reported a stake of 9.29% after an increase of 1.01 percentage points. Two consecutive trims followed: a drop of 1.07 percentage points to 8.22% on July 1, 2025, and a reduction of 1.03 percentage points to 7.19% on October 1, 2025.

The fund reversed direction at the start of 2026, expanding the holding by 2.17 percentage points to 9.36% on January 2, 2026. It then adjusted the position upward by 0.01 percentage points to 9.37% on March 10, 2026 while changing its holding purpose to a simple investment.

Since that March change, the stake has moved strictly downward. On July 1, 2026, the fund reported a decrease of 2.06 percentage points to 7.31%, and the October 2, 2026 report shaved off an additional 1.05 percentage points to reach 6.26%[1]. Across these two summer and autumn disclosures, the position shrank by more than three percentage points.

Exchange trading tallies show that between September 7, 2026 and October 7, 2026, foreign investors posted net selling of KRW -5 billion in Hyundai Wia, while domestic institutions as a whole registered net selling of KRW -1 billion[2].

What Triggers the Next Hyundai Wia Report?

The National Pension Service will only file another report on Hyundai Wia if its holding crosses another full percentage-point boundary or if it drops completely below the 5% statutory disclosure floor. If disposals continue at the recent pace, an exit below 5% would remove the holding from public reporting view entirely.

External markers offer the only upcoming public checkpoints for the auto parts maker. Analyst consensus expectations for Q3 2026 project Hyundai Wia revenue of KRW 2.3 trillion (≈$1.7 billion) and an operating profit of KRW 56 billion (≈$42 million), reflecting an operating margin of 2.5%[3]. That consensus estimate represents a 10% operating profit increase quarter on quarter compared to the actual Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 50 billion (≈$37 million), and a 7% increase year on year versus Q3 2025 operating profit of KRW 52 billion (≈$39 million)[3]. These analyst projections remain estimates, as Hyundai Wia has not yet filed its third-quarter financial results.
