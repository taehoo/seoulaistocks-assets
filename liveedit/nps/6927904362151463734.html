Which Korean companies did the fund buy on October 2, 2026?

In disclosures made on October 2, 2026, the National Pension Service increased its ownership in four separate listed firms, led by a 2.29 percentage point addition to Hyundaimarine&fireinsuranceco that brought its total stake to 11.71%[3]. The fund held 9,985,793 shares in the insurer after adding to a position that stood at 9.42% in July 2026 [D3, July 01 filing]. Over the 20 trading sessions through October 2, 2026, foreign investors purchased a net KRW 30 billion (≈$22 million) of the insurer's stock while overall domestic institutions were net sellers of KRW 9 billion (≈$7 million)[5].

Metals producer Hyundai Steel Company saw its stake raised by 1.0 percentage point to 9.84%, representing 13,134,604 shares held by the fund[4]. This accumulation reversed a drop recorded earlier in the year when the fund's position fell from 9.89% in April 2026 to 8.84% in July 2026 [April 01 filing, July 01 filing]. The holding purpose on record for Hyundai Steel Company remains general investment, a legal designation established on November 19, 2025, that allows the fund to reserve rights to engage on corporate governance matters [November 19, 2025 filing].

Holding company Hyundai G.f. Holdings and chemicals maker Foosung also received additional allocation. The pension fund lifted its stake in Hyundai G.f. Holdings by 1.02 percentage points to 6.02% across 11,043,443 shares[6]. Meanwhile, the stake in Foosung reached 6.22% following a 1.13 percentage point increase[1]. That mark follows a 0.32 percentage point rise to 5.09% reported on July 1, 2026 [July 01 filing]. Analysts expect Foosung to generate Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 190 billion (≈$142 million) and operating profit of KRW 24 billion (≈$18 million), though official corporate earnings reports have not yet been submitted[7]. Domestic Korean institutions bought a net KRW 29 billion (≈$22 million) of Foosung shares across the 20 trading sessions preceding October 2, 2026[5].

Where did the fund cut exposure across listed stocks?

Reductions hit four distinct issuers on October 2, 2026, including retailer Hotel Shilla, where the pension fund trimmed its holding by 0.93 percentage points down to 7.1%[2]. The lower stake leaves the fund holding 2,841,803 shares[2]. The trim marks a reversal from July 1, 2026, when the fund expanded its position to 8.03% [July 01 filing]. The current purpose on record for Hotel Shilla was downgraded to simple investment back on March 17, 2026, signalling a purely passive investment stance [March 17, 2026 filing]. Foreign investors sold a net KRW 34 billion (≈$25 million) of Hotel Shilla over the 20 trading sessions before the filing[5]. In parallel market moves, Korea Investment Management lowered its own stake in Hotel Shilla to 4.39% on September 8, 2026[8]. Market estimates put Hotel Shilla Q3 2026 revenue at KRW 979 billion (≈$729 million) and operating profit at KRW 62 billion (≈$46 million)[7].

Automotive parts maker Hyundai Wia saw its stake fall 1.05 percentage points to 6.26%, leaving the pension fund with 1,701,821 shares[9]. This sale extends a downward trend from July 1, 2026, when the fund chopped its stake by 2.06 percentage points to 7.31% [July 01 filing]. Hyundai Wia has been registered under a simple investment purpose since March 10, 2026 [March 10, 2026 filing].

The fund also decreased holdings in software publisher Hyundai Autoever and machinery manufacturer Hyundai Elevator. Hyundai Autoever was trimmed by 1.01 percentage points to 7.21% across 1,977,997 shares[10]. Its holding purpose has stood as simple investment since February 21, 2025 [February 21, 2025 filing]. Machinery producer Hyundai Elevator saw an identical reduction of 1.01 percentage points, taking the pension fund's remaining holding down to 9.27% across 3,625,527 shares[11]. These rebalancing trades formed part of a wider filing round that included non-subject holdings such as retail firm Hyundai Department Store and electronic component maker Haesung DS [Hyundai Department Store, Haesung DS filings].

What these filings leave unanswered

A single round of ownership filings cannot reveal the execution prices, underlying portfolio mandates, or sub-5% positions maintained by external asset managers. Because all disclosures were filed under the trade type of simple acquisition or disposal, these reports do not indicate active corporate governance campaigns[1][2][3]. Furthermore, because trades are published days after execution, market observers must look out for upcoming Q3 earnings reports and future regulatory updates on DART to verify if these position changes continued into the fourth quarter.

Questions people ask

Why did the National Pension Service file multiple stake changes on October 2, 2026?

Korean regulations require major shareholders to disclose net shifts in ownership once positions meet or cross statutory thresholds. On October 2, 2026, the fund reported routine adjustments across multiple companies under the simple acquisition or disposal designation.

What is the NPS holding purpose for Hyundai Steel Company?

The pension fund holds its stake in Hyundai Steel Company under a general investment purpose, which was updated on November 19, 2025. This classification allows the fund to engage on governance matters while maintaining its holding.
