Tracing the Eight Disclosures in Sequence

Under Korean market regulations, large shareholding disclosures are mandatory only when an investor holds 5% or more of a listed company's outstanding shares. Once an institutional position clears that line, subsequent moves of 1 percentage point or drops back beneath the floor trigger fresh reports to the market.

The sequence for Hana Materials shows exactly how this back-and-forth unfolds over multiple quarters.

2024-10-07: 4.29%

On October 7, 2024, the fund reported a 4.29% stake after a reduction of 2.2 percentage points, categorized as a simple acquisition or disposal. Four months later, on February 3, 2025, the fund surfaced back above the disclosure mark at 5.01%, up 0.02 percentage points under the same trade category. The fund continued adding shortly after, lifting the position on March 4, 2025 to 6.09%, an increase of 1.08 percentage points.

That peak did not last long.

On May 2, 2025, the fund dropped 1.16 percentage points to 4.93%, ducking back below the line through a simple acquisition or disposal. The position went quiet in the public record for eight months until January 2, 2026, when a filing recorded a 5.01% holding after a minor rise of 0.04 percentage points. On April 1, 2026, another build took the stake up 1.1 percentage points to 6.11%.

The retreat followed immediately. On May 6, 2026, NPS reported a stake of 5.0%, down 1.11 percentage points. Then came the latest report on June 1, 2026, logging a 1.29 percentage point cut down to 3.71%, leaving the fund with 734,082 shares[1]. Every single one of these trades was logged as a simple acquisition or disposal.

Status on Holding Intent

In Korean filings, the reason code for an individual trade—such as simple acquisition or disposal—merely describes the transaction mechanics. It is not the holding purpose. The statutory holding purpose itself governs governance posture: a simple investment designation indicates a passive holding, whereas general investment reserves the right to engage on corporate governance issues.

For Hana Materials, our filing summary does not show the holding purpose on record, though the underlying regulatory DART report itself contains one. What I can tell you from the transaction record is that none of the eight filings between late 2024 and mid-2026 registered a switch in intent. They were ordinary trading adjustments.

Elsewhere in the market, the fund does alter its posture when it intends to reserve broader rights. For example, during the same general window, the fund changed its purpose from simple investment to general investment in shipbuilder HD Hyundai Heavy Industries and chemicals maker KCC. Nothing of that sort has surfaced in this record.

Limits of the Trading Record

Nothing here settles whether these swings stem from dedicated stock-picking decisions or routine portfolio maintenance. The fund rebalances portfolios across internal and external mandates, tracks benchmark weights, and responds to broader asset allocation limits. An ebb and flow across the 5% threshold does not mean the fund has formed a fundamental thesis on the business.

Other market participants have shifted positions as well. In a separate large-shareholding report dated September 29, 2026, Samsung Asset Management disclosed a 2.44% stake in Hana Materials, down 2.84 percentage points[2]. For the 20 trading sessions running from August 31, 2026 to September 29, 2026, KRX flow data shows foreign investors recorded net buying of KRW 28 billion (≈$21 million), while Korean institutions across all categories logged net sales of KRW -29 billion[3].

I wouldn't interpret this filing sequence as a verdict on operational trajectory. Analyst consensus estimates for Q3 2026 project Hana Materials revenue at KRW 95 billion (≈$71 million) and operating profit at KRW 25 billion (≈$19 million), which would represent an operating margin of 26.0%[4]. That consensus estimate compares against Q2 2026 actual operating profit of KRW 26 billion (≈$19 million), implying a 5% quarter-on-quarter drop, but stands well above the Q3 2025 actual operating profit of KRW 12 billion (≈$9 million)[4]. Those are third-party analyst estimates, not completed quarterly results, which remain unfiled.

I will be monitoring whether the fund re-crosses the 5% disclosure boundary on any future accumulation. Because the position currently stands at 3.71%, any routine market transactions taking place below the threshold will remain entirely invisible to the public until another threshold filing is required[1].
